## What the pass taught

Looking back, half of the pass was worth its effort and half was not.

- **Worth it:** the two P1 on the home zones, adding an enrollment, and the approval page. Their complexity was far past the threshold, and the code is central or sensitive. The lasting gain on the approval page is its nine tests, more than the split.
- **Too much:** the three pages whose `build` was 56 to 65 lines. A widget tree of that length is not a defect to a Flutter developer. The work got breaks of each path and an emulator run, for layout that only moved. Its real gains were small: the enroll page's first tests, and one repeated block removed.
- **The cause:** the P2 were treated as a list to empty, although P2 already meant "act when the code is touched".

The rules that follow are the compass in [`code-quality.md`](../code-quality.md#compass-how-far-to-go). The next pass starts from it.
